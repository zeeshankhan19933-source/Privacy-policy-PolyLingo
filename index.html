1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile application PolyLingo ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"), developed and published by Zeeshan Khan. The App is a comprehensive language-learning platform designed to help users learn vocabulary, master grammar, practice pronunciation, take quizzes, and track learning progress across multiple languages.

By downloading, installing, or using PolyLingo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use the application.

2. Information We Collect and Process

We collect and handle the following categories of data to provide a seamless learning experience:

A. Learning Progress and App Preferences (Locally Stored)

  • Curriculum Progress: Completed lessons, unit milestones, unlocked levels, quiz scores, and accuracy rates.
  • Gamification Data: Daily learning streaks, earned experience points (XP), daily goal targets, and completion timestamps.
  • User Customizations: Target language selection, current proficiency level (Pre-A1 to C2), bookmarked vocabulary, and favorite flashcards.
  • App Preferences: Audio playback preferences, dark/light theme choices, and reminder settings.

Note: This information is stored securely on your local device (via modern Android DataStore and local database systems) and remains under your direct control.

B. Audio and Voice Data (Sensitive Permission)

PolyLingo includes interactive speaking practice, pronunciation guides, and conversation exercises.

  • Permission Requested: RECORD_AUDIO (Microphone Access).
  • Purpose: Used exclusively in real-time when you tap the microphone button to evaluate your pronunciation of words and phrases.
  • Zero Storage Guarantee: Your voice inputs are processed instantaneously for speech recognition scoring. We do not save, record, archive, sell, or profile your raw voice recordings. Voice audio is discarded immediately after the pronunciation score or transcription is produced.

C. Device and Diagnostics Information

  • Technical Telemetry: Device model, operating system version (Android OS), screen resolution, app version code, and crash logs generated automatically by Android OS / Google Play Services to diagnose unexpected errors and optimize stability.

3. Device Permissions We Request

PolyLingo requests only the minimal set of Android permissions strictly required for its core educational functions:

Permission Classification Reason & Functional Purpose
RECORD_AUDIO Runtime / Dangerous Required only when using the interactive pronunciation practice tool to capture voice samples for instant pronunciation scoring.
INTERNET Install-time / Normal Allows the app to fetch online language modules, download pronunciation audio, and connect to AI language tutoring services securely over HTTPS.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Install-time / Normal Monitors network connectivity to seamlessly adapt between offline study modes and online AI pronunciation features.
MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS Install-time / Normal Adjusts audio volume and output routing for natural speech playback during listening exercises.

4. Artificial Intelligence & Cloud Processing

PolyLingo incorporates advanced AI capabilities powered by Google Gemini API / Firebase AI Logic to generate adaptive language explanations, contextual vocabulary dialogues, and dynamic practice questions.

AI Data Safeguards:
  • Text prompts submitted for translation or grammar explanation are transmitted through encrypted HTTPS channels.
  • Your inputs are strictly utilized to respond to your educational queries in real time.
  • User data is never sold or shared with data brokers, nor is it used to build advertising profiles.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver certain app features, we rely on trusted industry-standard infrastructure providers that adhere to rigorous privacy standards:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, updates, base security verification, and platform integrity. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Gemini API / Google Cloud: Provides server-side intelligence for language translation, dialogue generation, and grammar guidance. (Google Cloud Privacy Notice)

These third-party providers have access to information solely to perform tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

6. Data Storage, Security & Retention

We implement comprehensive security measures designed to safeguard your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmissions between the App and remote services use Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2/1.3) with standard SSL/HTTPS certificates.
  • Local Sandbox: On-device learning records are stored in private internal application storage, inaccessible to other apps installed on your device.
  • Data Retention: Since learning metrics (XP, streak, lesson completion) are stored on your local device, they persist until you clear the app data or uninstall the application.

7. Data Deletion and User Control

You have full autonomy over your data:

  1. Clear Progress in App: You can reset your learning progress, vocabulary bookmarks, and study statistics at any time through the in-app Settings screen.
  2. Device Settings: You can permanently wipe all local application storage by going to Android Settings > Apps > PolyLingo > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  3. Permission Revocation: You can grant or revoke Microphone access at any time via Android Settings > Apps > PolyLingo > Permissions.
  4. Deletion Requests: If you have any inquiries regarding data deletion or wish to verify that no residual data exists, you can email us directly at zeeshankhan19933@gmail.com. We will process your request within 30 days.
